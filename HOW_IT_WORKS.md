# Ad Template Studio — at a glance

**Full write-up for office sharing:** [docs/AD_TEMPLATE_STUDIO.md](docs/AD_TEMPLATE_STUDIO.md)

You describe a product (photo + title + optional copy and prices). The studio researches the product, plans several ad directions, builds layouts with your real image, and shows ranked previews you can download or refine in chat.

The doc is written for a **general audience first**; a **technical appendix** at the end covers pipeline steps, APIs, and file outputs for engineers.
